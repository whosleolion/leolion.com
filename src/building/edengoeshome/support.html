What Your Gift Supports

Bringing a new musical from an idea to a fully staged production takes the work of many artists and collaborators. Your gift directly supports the people who make that work possible.

Campaign funds will help us compensate the artists and creative team behind Eden Goes Home, while maintaining equal pay, union compliance, and the high production value that this project deserves. Your contribution helps make sure that every part of the process, from the performers onstage to the designers, musicians, technicians, and creative team behind the scenes, is treated as essential to the creation of the show.

By supporting Eden Goes Home, you aren’t simply helping us put on a performance. You’re helping an original musical take its next step toward a larger life.

Our Goals for This Project

This production is an important milestone in Eden Goes Home’s development. With your support, we hope to:

Fully realize a staged version of the musical, bringing the world of the Summers family to life through performance, design, music, and movement.

Test new material and songs with a live audience, discovering what resonates and what can be strengthened through the experience of performance.

Gauge audience and industry interest in the musical and its potential for a larger-scale production.

Gather reviews, feedback, and constructive critique that will help us continue developing the show beyond Winterfest.

Every new musical needs opportunities to grow in front of an audience. This production gives Eden Goes Home that opportunity. It is both a destination and a beginning: a chance to share the story we have built so far, learn from the people who experience it, and take what we learn into the show’s next chapter.
