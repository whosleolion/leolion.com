Why give

Campaign funds compensate the artists and creative team behind Eden Goes Home, while maintaining equal pay, union compliance, and the high production value this project deserves. Your gift also helps cover crew, instrumentalists, props, costumes, projections and rehearsal space.

The goal is $15,000. Every gift, large or small, moves Eden Goes Home closer to opening night.

What this production is for

Fully realize it

Gauge interest

Audience and industry response to a larger-scale production.

Keep growing

Reviews and feedback that carry the show beyond Winterfest.
