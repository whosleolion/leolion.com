What is Eden Goes Home?

When Trinity College Dublin student Eden Summers unexpectedly has to move back ‘home’ to her small town in the U.S. due to her grandmother’s illness, she struggles to navigate balancing family, friends, and school with her dream of a bigger future. Exploring themes of generational tension, socio-economic status, mental health, and national identity, Eden Goes Home is a moving new musical by Lena Streitwieser.

Description by Bella Littler.
