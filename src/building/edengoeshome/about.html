Eden Goes Home

About the show

A new musical about family, memory, and the long way home.

The story

Eden Summers is a student at Trinity College Dublin when she is called back to her small American hometown: her grandmother’s dementia is getting worse, and someone has to come home.

Back in the house she grew up in, Eden is pulled between family obligations, old friendships and her education, and the bigger life she was building. Her mother Julie gave up dreams of her own. Her stepfather George is stretched thin by money worries. Her grandmother wants, more than anything, to be understood.

The musical asks: who are you when “home” isn’t home?

Authenticity or fitting in: Eden has to decide which one she can live without.

Generational tensionSocio-economic struggleMental healthNational identityAuthenticity vs. conformity

Where it came from

Lena Streitwieser started writing Eden Goes Home after spontaneously composing lyrics in her dorm room at Trinity College Dublin, inspired by her own experience of moving back to America.

From that first song it has grown into a full musical, written, composed and directed by Lena.
