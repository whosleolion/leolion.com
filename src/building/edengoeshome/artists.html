Lena Streitwieser

L

Writer · Composer · Director

Lena Streitwieser has written, composed, and is currently developing an original musical, titled Eden Goes Home, inspired by her experience moving back to the U.S. after living abroad in Dublin, Ireland as a student at Trinity College Dublin.
