The Artists

Crew

Lena Streitwieser

Director, Musical Director, Writer/Composer, Eden Summers

Lena Streitwieser is a storyteller based in New York City. She is a recent graduate of the Dual BA with Trinity College Dublin and Columbia University, having studied film and music. Her work as a film director has been awarded in festivals from California to Japan. She has published film reviews, festival reviews, and essays, though she writes most consistently in her diary. Living in Berlin, Washington, D.C., Dublin, Chicago, and New York inspired her creatively. Eden Goes Home is her first musical. Eternal thanks to my incredible family, friends, and this amazing cast for helping my dreams come true! www.lenastreitwieser.com

SD

Sonja Dighe

Producer, Stage Manager

Sonja Dighe is a theatre producer and administrator who works across multiple international theatre venues and festivals. She has brought shows to Dublin, Edinburgh, Prague, London, Washington D.C., Philadelphia, San Francisco, Kansas City, Seattle, L.A., and Austin. In NYC she’s developed shows at The Tank, A.R.T. NY, MCC, Downtown Art, and BAH. Sonja supports original musicals, fringe shows, and interactive experiences, such as Edinburgh Horror Festival’s Best Musical Ghosted! A New Musical, Help Me!!!!, Kaddish (How to be a Sanctuary), and Liar’s Night. She is co-founder of Helianthus Productions and associate producer at The Firebird Project.

XM

Xavier Miller

Orchestrator

Xavier Miller is a Composer and Musician from Washington D.C., and is currently based in Pittsburgh, PA. He is currently studying to get a Bachelor’s Degree in Music Composition from the University of Pittsburgh. Xavier has performed in venues such as The Kennedy Center, The Department of Education, and Universal Studios. He has also composed, arranged, and orchestrated works for orchestra, concert band, Pop/Rock bands, and much more. He is very excited to be working as part of the crew for Eden Goes Home!

MM

Makena Margolin

Movement Coordinator/Choreographer

Makena Margolin is a New York and Irish-based interdisciplinary theatre director, award-winning playwright, and lyricist. She holds a BA from Trinity College Dublin in Drama and Theatre Studies, minoring in Classical Civilization, and trained at the Orange County School of the Arts (MT Conservatory). Directing credits include the acclaimed “Ghosted! A New Musical” (Edinburgh Fringe, 2025), “Carrie The Musical” (O’Reilly Theatre, 2024), “Wretched Dogs,” an Iliad retelling (Samuel Beckett Theatre, 2025), “Serials” (The Tank, Feb 2026), “The Lightning Thief Musical” (Trinity Halls, 2023). Authorships include award-winning “#143 Westchester Road” (TILDA Prize, 2025) and “Fly Away with Me” (TheTank’s Limefest, 2026). www.makenamargolin.com

OM

Órla Meyers

Piano, Band Lead

Órla is the pianist for Eden Goes Home. She is a senior in the Dual BA program between Sciences Po Paris and Columbia University. Órla studies Political Science but music is where most of her energy goes these days. She grew up immersed in both NYC’s classical music scene and the rich traditional music of Ireland, where her parents now live. This summer, she wrote and recorded original music, a challenging but exciting experience. She feels lucky to have been exposed to a wide variety of music genres over the years, and even luckier that she gets to add musical theatre to that list with Eden Goes Home!
