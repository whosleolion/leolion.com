Quick start

How do I price something?
Type it, like 5 healing potions in a village, or tap the + button and pick buy or sell, a kind, then an item.
How do I price a whole shopping trip?
Type a list, like 3 rope, 2 torches, a lantern, and add it to the cart. Use Haggle in the cart when the party bargains.
It doesn't know my item.
Type it anyway. The app guesses from tags you can adjust. Add it to the cart once, or tap Remember it to save it as your own item.
Can I change the prices for my world?
Yes. In Settings, World, type a fact such as a healing potion costs 40 gp, or start a custom world from one of the calibrated worlds.
Is my data backed up?
Your data lives on your device. Use Settings, Back up and share to copy a world out as text, and Load pasted world to bring it back.
Does it need an internet connection?
No. It works fully offline and never sends your data anywhere.

About the prices

Prices are estimates meant to feel right at a game table. Real-world prices are approximate (a typical mid-size US city, about 2025) and fantasy and sci-fi prices are original to Fool's Gold, except SRD equipment, which uses the published SRD 5.1 prices.
