Fool's Gold Privacy Policy

A game tool by Firebird Games. Last updated November 14, 2026.

The short version

Fool's Gold does not collect, send, sell or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Everything you type and save stays on your device.

What the app stores, and where

The app saves your worlds, custom items, custom words, carts, recent searches and settings on your device only (in the app's local storage). It never uploads them. You can copy a world out as text from Settings, Back up and share, and you can erase everything with Reset everything.

Dictation (optional)

If you tap the microphone button, the app listens only while the button is lit and turns what you say into text in the price box. On an iPhone this uses Apple's speech recognition, which may process audio on the device or on Apple's servers under Apple's privacy terms. In a web browser it uses the browser's own speech service. Fool's Gold never records, stores or receives your audio, and it works exactly the same if you never use the microphone or deny permission.

Words it did not know

When you ask for something the app has no price for, it keeps that phrase in a short list on your device so you can see it. The list never leaves your device unless you tap Share the list in Settings and choose where to send it.

What the app does not do

  • It does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos or camera. It asks for the microphone and speech recognition only if you tap the dictation button.
  • It does not include third-party analytics, advertising or crash-reporting code.
  • It does not make network requests of its own while you use it. Its fonts and price lists are included in the app, and it works offline. (Dictation, if you use it, is handled by your phone's or browser's speech service.)

The website version

If you open Fool's Gold in a web browser at leolion.com, your browser loads the app from our host. Like any website, the host may keep ordinary server logs (such as your IP address and the time of the request). We do not use those logs to identify you. Once loaded, the web version behaves like the app: your data stays in your browser.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children. It is a price calculator for tabletop and role-playing games.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
