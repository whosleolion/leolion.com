Foxwood Wilds

Tap a hex to reveal or re-fog it. Player view hides everything under fog; GM view shows the hidden art at half strength.

Foxwood Wild Magic: Open Connection

When you surge, the magic opens a thread between you and a type of fae. The connection lasts 1 hour unless a line says otherwise. A save DC is 13 unless stated. To snip the thread early, touch cold iron to it and succeed on a DC 13 Intelligence (Arcana) or Intelligence (Religion) check.

Map of Foxwood Wilds

Map of Foxwood Wilds

Wondrous item, uncommon

This hand-drawn map of a Feywild region is divided into 19 numbered hexes, each hidden behind drifting fog.

Investigate. Studying the map takes 1 hour. Choose up to three hexes and make an Intelligence (Arcana) or Intelligence (Religion) check. On a 20 or higher, one chosen hex clears of fog; on a 25 or higher, two; on a 30 or higher, three. On a lower result, nothing clears. Cleared hexes stay revealed.

Fey Interference. Each time you study the map again before the next dawn, roll a d20. On a 10 or lower, the map's magic lashes out and you trigger a wild magic surge (roll on the Foxwood wild magic table).
