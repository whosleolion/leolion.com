932 Madison St

The block as the city's own records draw it: every footprint, roof height, curb, hydrant and street tree, under the elevated tracks on Broadway.

What you're looking at

A model of the blocks around 932 Madison St, generated from public records. Positions and sizes are real; surface detail is an informed reconstruction.

From the records

  • Building footprints, roof heights, floors, year built and use: NYC Building Footprints and PLUTO (NYC Open Data)
  • Sidewalks, pavement edges and street centrelines: NYC Planimetric Database and CSCL
  • Street trees with species and trunk size: NYC Parks Forestry Tree Points
  • Hydrants: NYC DEP
  • Rooftops and back yards: New York State orthoimagery

Reconstructed

  • Facades are procedural, matched by building class and age to what street photos of these blocks show. Individual windows, doors and shop names are not the real ones.
  • The elevated structure follows Broadway's real centreline; its steelwork is modelled from photos.
  • Parked cars, lamp posts and yard trees are placed by rule.
