She was the co-founder of (link: "Skunkpets")[(set: $navPending to "Skunkpets")(display: "func-navigate")], a virtual pet website that was active online 2001-2009. <h3>Career</h3>\ Krysina attended St. Petersburg University in Russia 1997-1999 and transfered to Ellory College in Pennsylvania for 1999-2001 to complete an undergraduate computer science degree. While at Ellory, Krysina (known online as "kraska1") published small HTML-based games such as "Bad Door". Krysina's game design career took off when she and (link: "Nick Galveston")[(set: $navPending to "Nick Galveston")(display: "func-navigate")] launched SkunkPets in 2001, and especially following the (link: "2004 NetCon")[(set: $navPending to "NetCon 2004")(display: "func-navigate")] convention where she was celebrated as "an architect for the next wave of online gaming". In 2005, Interactive Periodical cited her as a "changemaker for online spaces" — one of "the 50 artists, actors, authors, activists and icons who are making the world a more stimulating place".
