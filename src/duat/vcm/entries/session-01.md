::: leo
A mob deal at [[Dive's Bistro]] brings together four unlikely companions:

**[[Finnean]]**, a halfling wizard in over his head, is working to pay off a debt to bloodthirsty seafood chef **[[Orson Dive]]**. **[[Cassandra]]**, an elven socialite with a magical secret, is being held captive in the bistro's cooler. With her is **[[Olf]]**, a bumbling homunculus foisted upon her by her parents. **[[Rosie]]**, an outlander ranger brought to the city after work dried up in the wild, is doing short-term security detail for a Dwarven wizard called **[[Alamir Koldovich]]**. Koldovich has come to Vista City to sell [[Necromantic Scrolls|necromantic spell scrolls]] to the orcish mafia, represented by **[[Pious & Reverent Henchman|Pious and Reverent Henchman]]**, twin brothers and mob enforcers. The Henchmen have brought along **[[Felt]]**, a gnomish rogue, to help them pull a mimic-suitcase scam on Koldovich.

Just as the deal went sour (and Cassandra made her escape), a large-scale magical explosive went off in the building, caving in the Bistro. Felt, Rosie and Finnean all took the opportunity to make off with the cargo and cash, while Cassandra blasted her way into the outdoors. A fight ensued in the sewer tunnel below the Bistro (used by Dive in the past for running mob goods), awaking a Skeleton that had been lying dormant in the sewer muck. Cassandra disguised herself as a kitchen maid to momentarily throw off the [[City Watch|city watch]]. Olf quickly blew her cover, and she was forced to escape back into the collapsed restaurant, but not before grabbing the real (non-mimic) scroll-case from the hands of the city watch.

In the sewer tunnel, Felt was knocked unconscious in the combat, and nearly left for dead in the arms of the skeleton as Rosie and Finnean fought over the cash. Protecting himself from Rosie, Finnean became ensnared by the mimic he had accidentally stolen. Cassandra escaped into the tunnel and blew up the entrance, closing off the path for the city watch to follow. She distracted the Skeleton, who Rosie then killed with a radiant spell, and the two of them returned to stabilize and heal Felt.

With a tenuous peace in the air between this accidental dungeon-crawling party, Finnean (still ensnared despite his best efforts) hears more clattering of bones down the sewer hallway. And of course, the way back is caved in, so the only way out is through.
