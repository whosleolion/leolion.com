The prison's moving magic tower, which (the crew assumes) holds all the prisoners and items taken from the [[Underborough]]. Its warden is [[Sir Irving Spear]]. Raided in two parts: the crew took the prison, freed [[Rug Shagley|Rug]], [[The Moth]], [[Solomon Grundy]] and a very cool pirate lady, and captured [[Carpet Kelly|Carpet]].
