**The Illyrian Lands** are the modern remnants of Ancient Telos, the venerated Elven empire that once held claim to the entire West. The Illyrian national union of High Elven states is ruled by a dynastic empire, whose power is concentrated in [[Aster Valley]], the home of **New Telos,** a city built in the late Third Regal Age to commemorate the Telosian legacy lost to the Dragons. The founding of New Telos and the formation of the imperial union both militarily and ideologically bolstered the Illyrian side of the Dragon Wars. Illyrium was a major player in the Dragon Wars and alongside Hygard and Tharan'dul led many decisive victories against the Dragons, largely in Jogunheim and Rag'dul.
