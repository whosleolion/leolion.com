On the [[Kainos]] map: the eastern land, with [[Journeyforge]] on its north coast, Holdov, the Farhaul Mountains, and the Arkenrail. The Quinn Mountain Line ends at Journeyforge.

Its capital is [[Journeyforge]]. [[Yin Illethar]] is imprisoned here.
