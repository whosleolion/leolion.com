> “Vathe Mera Mia Kainos Geos” / “Every day, a new world.”
> — Ancient Elven Proverb of Telos

**Kainos** is the Material Plane, located at the intersection of Four Elemental Planes: Fire, Stone, Water, and Wind. The four “corners” of the world extend out into their respective Planes, at points known as the Four Great Gates. Millenia ago, the primordial clashing of elements created a new kind of world—a place of fusion and contradiction, not eternal and unchanging like the elements, but forever shifting in conflict with itself… Here, what were once pure elements became more than the sum of their parts: *material form*, capable of change, of being created and destroyed. It is a world of impermanence, changing by the minute, perpetually new and eternally fleeting. Eventually, all forms crumble, and the essences within them take new shapes. Such is the way of material things: “*Every day, a new world.*”

In those parts of Kainos not yet eclipsed by a thick layer of smog, you can still see the stars. In the centuries following the Dragon Wars, the legendary days of sword and sorcery have become nothing but distant memory. Now, bustling industrial cities crowd the coastlines and magic trains run thunderously through the mountain ranges. But though the fearsome dragons of legend have been slain, the forces of evil never rest... they only take on new faces.

## Ages of Kainos

**Primordial Era.** A period of planar fusion, during which the world was formed. The only humanoid cultures known to exist during this time are the Genasi of the Great Gates, cross-planar migrations of elementals who ventured into the “elemental chaos” of the still-forming material realm. End of period marked by the solidifying of the isolated Material Plane during and around 1000 AE, as described in an ancient Dwarven story of "cooling ores."

**Astral Era.** A period of antiquity marked by the rise and fall of the first Material-native humanoid civilizations: the **Nymphs** of Illyria and the **Vaettir** of Dologoth. These ancient peoples (who would eventually become Elves, Orcs, and Dwarves) discovered the first mortal magics and founded the first major nations of the Material plane. In honor of their star-based calendars, these cultures are historically known as the **Astral Peoples**, and their inventions and conflicts laid the groundwork for centuries of humanoid history to come.

- **Bronze Age (1001 – 651 AE).** The dawn of material civilizations. The earliest known settlements of mountain-born Vaettir and woodland Nymphs date back to this period. Early advances in mortal magic.
- **Iron Age (650 – 211 AE).** The epoch of ancient war. Advances in magic among newly varied and widespread humanoid populations bring about a series of increasingly destructive military conflicts known as the **Astral Wars**. Trade records from this period also contain the first recorded mention of a third new humanoid origin-group: the **Fáuna**, or beast-like (Tabaxi, Aarakocra, Sahuagin, Satyr, Loxodon).
- **Venerable Age (212 – 1 AE).** The cradle of prosperity. The last Astral War was resolved peacefully by three newly-emerged world powers: **Elves** of **Telos**, **Dwarves** of **Farheim**, and **Orcs** of **Unarith** — the Three Stars of Kainos. A bountiful period of harmony marked by the flourishing of arts, culture, and arcane knowledge.

As the Astral Era drew to a close, the stars above were shrouded in smoke. Telos, Farheim, and Unarith, legendary strongholds that had solidly withstood the force of many arcane wars, were laid to ruin by a new threat: Dragons. Previously only a distant legend, glimpsed fleetingly as shadows in the sky, the great Wyrms came out of hiding, drawn to the wealth and splendor of the Three Stars, and within only a year, turned the great cities of old to ash.

**Solar Era.** A period of great change and crisis brought by the fall of the Astral powers, marked by the rise of the first new peoples to appear in Kainos in several centuries: the **Humans** of **Hygard**. Ascending from the ashes of the Draconic Cataclysm, the Humans quickly became the preeminent power in Kainos, despite possessing no innate magic of their own. In general, the Era is defined by the conflict with the Draconic threat, conquest and expansion by massive imperial powers, and the decline in folk magics in favor of industrialized arcana.

- **Sunless Age (0 – 470 SE).** Desolation after the cataclysm. The few surviving documents of this age record the scattering of Elves and Orcs across their respective landmasses, the mass migration of Dwarves from the ruins of Farheim to a new home by the Stone Gate in Tharan’dul, and the rise of a fledgling nation of Hygard on the great southwestern plateau.
- **Silver Age (470 – 990 SE).** Feudal conquest, monster-slaying, birth of the ‘adventurer.’ The quickly-growing Hygard began an age-long anti-draconic crusade known as the **Dragon Wars**: Forging alliances with Illyria and Tharan’dul, they sought to drive dragons from the ‘sunlit realms’ into Dologoth, and then to exterminate them entirely.
- **Golden Age (991 – 1219).** The aftermath of the Dragon Wars. After centuries of conquest, Hygard and its allies achieved the total **extinction** of dragonkind in Kainos. Dologothi peoples like Unarith-descended Orcs and Goblins, who had largely opposed the crusade in favor of peaceful resolution with the dragons, were considered anathema by the newly-thriving Sunlit Realms, and violent conquest and occupation of Dologothi land continued for over a century.
- **Brass Age (1220 – 1252/Present).** The rise of modernity. A united congress of Dologothi peoples drafted the **Reformational Constitution**, swearing allegiance to the Sunlit Realms and retroactively condemning their past opposition to the Dragon Wars. This document was ratified by a newly-formed global political body, the **Grand Kainos National Union**, which outlawed all international ‘adventuring’ in recognized nations under the **Questing Accords**. With rapid advances in arcane technology, major economic and political shifts, and the rise of high-speed transit, the dawn of the Brass Age saw the reforging of Kainos into a newly industrialized world.
