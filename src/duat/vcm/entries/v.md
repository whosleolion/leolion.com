::: leo
A still-animated skeleton found in the abandoned sewer lab, who identified himself as "Five" and only seemed interested in solving a rubik's cube. [[Felt]] was strongly opposed to leaving him behind.
:::

::: thomas
A little skeleton boy the crew found in an abandoned lab in the sewers. Smart and nonviolent. Later ran off as "Johnny 5", and the crew tracked him to [[The Colosseum]], where [[Dissector]] had him. Created by [[The Necromancer]].
:::
