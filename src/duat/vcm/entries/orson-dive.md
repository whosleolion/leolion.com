A bloodthirsty seafood chef. His old crime den, built into a dungeon chamber of the sewers under the Bistro, held a necromantic lab (likely not run by Dive himself).

Ran [[Dive's Bistro]] and oversaw the scroll deal. [[Finnean]] owed him a gambling debt. He was working for the mystery villain. His skeleton later turned up with the zombie skeleton army on the ship.
