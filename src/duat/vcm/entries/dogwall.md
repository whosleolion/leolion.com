---
title: Dogwall
type: district
summary: The walled slum south of the river, ringed by the Dogwall wall.
---
The wall has gates on the north, west, east and south sides. Inside are the [[Siege Cistern]] and the walled slum itself. The Freight Bypass loops around the outside of the wall so that freight never has to go through it.

Neighbors: [[The Smogs]] to the west, [[Southbank]] to the east, and [[The Spoils]] across the river to the north.
