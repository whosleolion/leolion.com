::: leo
In the tunnel below [[Dive's Bistro]], our party discovered Dive's disused crime den, built out of a dungeon chamber in the city's sewer system. By the light of the arcane torches, they investigated the den, which contained a disgusting couch (with a skeletal hand in the cushions which tried to tear up [[Felt]]'s leg), as well as a series of small rooms and closets. The largest room contained a long-disused necromantic lab which was being used to develop skeletons capable of completing increasingly complex tasks. It seems whoever was running the lab (likely not Dive himself) was forced to abandon it in a hurry a few months ago.

From one of the supply closets, Felt outfitted himself with a backpack full of [[Spellwash]]: industrial arcane residue used for bootlegging magic.

Also in the lab was a still-animated skeleton who identified himself as "**[[Five]]**", who only seemed interested in solving a rubik's cube. The party cautiously befriended him. Despite some initial reservations, Felt was strongly opposed to leaving him alone in the abandoned lab, and the party agreed to take him along. Looking for a place to stash him, lie low following the bombing of Dive's Bistro, and figure out what to do with their stolen cargo, they set out to find an [[Underborough]] Rail stop to take them to [[Underton]].

As they arrived at the Rail Station, they failed to notice somebody scrying on the location of the stolen scrollcase from afar.
