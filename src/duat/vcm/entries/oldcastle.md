Oldcastle Palace: the Hygardian capital citadel, constructed off of the Telosian channel in the Third Regal Age by Queen Clarita Vistal. Vista City grew up around it.

It sits on the high ground above [[The Bustle]], with [[Beholder Park]] to the east and [[The Spoils]] below to the south. The [[Castle Ramp]] climbs up to it from the river.
